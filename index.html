About ElaraPay

ElaraPay is a fully functional, high-fidelity application built as a portfolio showcase and educational demonstration of premium fintech product design and front-end engineering.

No real money is transferred, stored, or processed at any point. All balances, transactions, and financial data exist solely within your browser's local storage on your personal device and are never transmitted to any server.

This application is intended strictly for non-illegal, non-fraudulent, and non-deceptive use. It must not be used to misrepresent financial capability, deceive third parties, or facilitate any form of fraud or financial crime.

ElaraPay is not a licensed financial institution, payment service provider, or regulated entity under any jurisdiction. It does not hold, transmit, or process real currency of any kind.

By using this application you acknowledge and agree to these terms. Built with care in Nigeria.
